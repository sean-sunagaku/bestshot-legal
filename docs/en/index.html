From a short video to three favorites

BestShot is an iPhone app that picks three photos from a short video to match the moment you want to capture. Your original video and selected photos are saved in the app’s library.

You choose when images are sent

Photo selection uses AI. First give separate consent to AI use and transfer to Cloudflare and OpenAI. We then ask before each video and send up to 12 reduced candidate images, your photo idea and related data. Review, change or withdraw consent in app Settings.

Current availability

BestShot is currently in beta. Account-enabled versions use Sign in with Apple, a free allowance and BestShot Pro. Older or local test versions may use invitation access codes. Available features and prices appear in your app.
