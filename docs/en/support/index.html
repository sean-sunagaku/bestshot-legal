Record or import a video

Record a five-second video or choose a video of up to 30 seconds from Photos. Choose the kind of moment you want or enter your own selection instructions.

Check BestShot permissions in iPhone Settings if you have trouble with the camera, microphone, or saving to Photos. Recording without audio is available if microphone access is denied.

AI selection and connection errors

AI selection needs an internet connection and either Apple sign-in or an invitation access code, depending on your app version. You never need to enter an OpenAI API key. Candidate images are sent after separate AI transfer consent and confirmation for each video.

For an access-code error, check your invitation instructions. For usage-limit or timeout errors, wait and retry. Beta usage limits may change with service operation.

To avoid image transfer, cancel before sending or withdraw under AI use and transfer consent in Settings. New versions have no device-only selection mode. You can continue browsing and managing media already stored in the library.

Save, share, or delete

Your three selected photos and original video are saved in the app library. From the results screen, you can export to Photos or use iOS sharing.

Use Delete in a recording’s library menu to remove its three photos and original video from the app. Copies in Photos or other services remain. See Delete your data.

Charges and languages

Account-enabled versions allow up to three free AI selections per day or up to thirty with BestShot Pro, resetting at 00:00 UTC. Pro is an auto-renewing monthly subscription. Check the price, period and renewal terms on Apple’s purchase screen. No free trial, annual plan or Family Sharing is offered. A purchase occurs only when you confirm it with Apple. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple account settings. Deleting the app or BestShot account does not cancel a subscription.

This website supports Japanese, English, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, and Korean. New app versions support Japanese, English, Simplified Chinese and Korean. Changing the website language does not change the app language.
