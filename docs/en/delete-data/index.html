Photos and videos on your device

  • Open the BestShot library and choose Delete from the recording’s menu. Confirm to remove that recording’s three photos and original video from the app.
  • Delete exported copies separately in Photos. Check Recently Deleted, iCloud Photos, and device backup settings. Shared copies must also be handled at their destination.
  • Stop future AI transfers by cancelling before transfer or withdrawing in Settings under AI use and transfer consent. Change camera, microphone and Photos permissions in iOS Settings. Withdrawal does not reverse earlier processing.

Delete your account and server records

Choose account deletion in the plan/account screen, reauthenticate with Apple, and confirm retention details and the need to cancel subscriptions separately. If disconnected, check deletion status and reauthenticate with the same Apple account when required. Necessary recovery information remains until completion. Completion receipts expire after one day and replay-prevention records after ten minutes; maintenance erases them after expiry. Re-registering after deletion resets that day’s free allowance.

Deleting an app or account does not stop an Apple subscription. Cancel it separately in Apple settings; see Apple subscription cancellation. Manage copies in Photos, shared services and backups separately.

Request server deletion

Email us below with the subject BestShot data deletion request. Include the approximate time and time zone of the online selection and only the minimum additional information you can share to identify it. A selection ID is helpful if available, but is not displayed in the app and is not required.

Account-enabled versions can first use account deletion in the app. Older unlinked records may need additional confirmation or may not be uniquely identifiable. We check the requester and records before deletion and explain the result or any limitation.

Do not send access codes, OpenAI API keys, original videos, face photos, or identity documents.

sunagaku.dx@gmail.com

Scope and limits

The server stores selection instructions and results, usage, authentication and subscription-check records; it does not store candidate images or videos in the database. Account deletion removes the linked records. We accept requests for older unlinked records where the target can be identified.

OpenAI, Cloudflare, email providers, Apple, and shared destinations have their own retention, backup, and legal requirements. We will explain legal or security retention needs and technical limits on immediate deletion where relevant. See the privacy policy for details.
