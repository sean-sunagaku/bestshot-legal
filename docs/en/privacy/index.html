Operator and scope

sunagaku, the developer and operator of BestShot, provides this policy for information handled by the BestShot iOS app, its online selection service, and this website. Contact us at the email address below.

sunagaku.dx@gmail.com

Information on your device

  • Recorded or imported videos, extracted photos, photo ideas, selection results, dates and library history stay in the app storage on your device. Videos may include audio when microphone permission is granted.
  • Access codes and account credentials are stored in device Keychain. Terms and AI transfer consent versions, dates, destinations and settings are stored on the device. Environment-specific apps have separate storage and endpoints.
  • Photos are exported to Photos or shared with other apps when you choose those actions. Device data may be included in Apple backups depending on your iOS and backup settings.

Information sent for online AI selection

After you agree on the confirmation screen, up to 12 reduced-size JPEG candidate images, selection instructions including free text, frame IDs, elapsed positions within the video, and on-device quality scores are sent through the BestShot server to OpenAI. The complete original video, its audio, and full-resolution extracted photos are not sent for AI selection.

Images may contain faces, backgrounds, and visible text. Ensure you have the rights and any required permission from the people shown or their guardians before recording or sending them. Avoid including sensitive information in images or instructions.

AI selection requires separate consent to AI use and transfers to Cloudflare and OpenAI. Accepting the terms alone does not authorize a transfer. You can cancel before each video. Withdrawal in Settings, or a change to the destination or disclosure version, requires fresh consent before further transfers. Previously processed or transmitted information cannot be recalled by this action.

Server records and purposes

The BestShot database stores selection IDs, instructions, provider and model names, candidate counts, selected frame IDs, ranks, scores, creation times and usage records. We use these to provide selection, handle faults and prevent abuse or excessive use. New versions do not generate or show written selection reasons.

Candidate images, original videos, and access codes are not stored in this database. Images are handled temporarily during transmission and processing. Normal application logging does not print request bodies or OpenAI error bodies.

Account-enabled versions process the Apple user identifier, BestShot account ID, authentication and replay-prevention data, account usage and subscription information based on Apple-signed transactions. Apple tokens needed for authorization refresh and account-deletion revocation are encrypted on the server. Apple credentials may contain an email address. We do not ask you to separately enter your name or email, track you with advertising identifiers, or sell your information.

Service providers and international processing

  • OpenAI processes candidate images and instructions to return selection results. We send store: false to the Responses API; this does not disable all retention. Abuse monitoring, image safety checks, and other exceptions follow OpenAI’s API data controls and applicable service settings.
  • Cloudflare provides API networking, execution, and database infrastructure. Connection information such as IP addresses may be processed for infrastructure operation and security. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub records visitor IP addresses for security. We do not add advertising, analytics scripts, or cookie-based tracking to this site. See the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Apple provides sign-in, app distribution, in-app purchases and auto-renewing subscriptions, transaction verification, feedback you submit, Photos and device backups. The Apple privacy policy applies.
  • Support emails include your reply address and the content you provide; we use them to respond and verify requests as needed. These providers and email infrastructure may process information outside Japan or your country of residence, subject to their safeguards and applicable law.

Retention

Local history, photos, and videos remain until you delete them. Copies exported to Photos or shared elsewhere must be managed separately. Backup retention and restoration follow the relevant service settings.

Account-linked server records have no routine automatic deletion deadline. Account deletion erases linked selection metadata, usage, subscription checks and credentials. Encrypted recovery data needed for incomplete Apple revocation or deletion remains for retries. The completion receipt expires after one day and pseudonymous replay-prevention records after ten minutes; maintenance erases them after expiry. The app shows the applicable retention before deletion. Unlinked records from older access-code versions may remain until an identifiable deletion request is processed.

Providers such as OpenAI, backups, and security records have their own retention policies and legal requirements. Immediate erasure of every copy is not guaranteed. Support correspondence is retained for handling requests and necessary recordkeeping; you may request deletion of information no longer needed.

Withdrawal, deletion, and your rights

Stop future AI transfers by cancelling before transfer or withdrawing in Settings under AI use and transfer consent. Change camera, microphone and Photos permissions in iOS Settings. Withdrawal does not reverse earlier processing.

Delete device media in the library. In account-enabled versions, request server account deletion in the app and reauthenticate with Apple. Account deletion is separate from cancelling an Apple subscription. See Delete your data.

Email us about access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection, or receipt and transfer of your data where applicable law grants these rights. We will check applicable conditions and verify the requester and relevant records. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Minors, security, and changes

Minors should obtain parental or guardian permission where required by law. BestShot is not offered as a service directed at children. Contact us if you have concerns about a child’s information.

Safeguards include encrypted transport, separate APIs, databases and credentials for each environment, Apple authentication or access-code controls, encrypted stored Apple tokens, and no public shared-history API. Take care when sharing a device, exporting photos or sharing with other services.

We update this page when features or data practices change. Material changes will be announced in the app or on this site, and we will seek consent in advance where a new processing activity requires it.
